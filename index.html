User guide

A quick walkthrough of the planner and what to enter.

1. Set up your programme

Enter your programme duration, current study year, and current period. The planner keeps your information in this browser.

2. Choose how to enter results

Course marks: Add each period, choose its type, choose a year for Semester 1 or 2, then enter each course's credit units and final mark. Mark audited courses so they are left out of GPA and counted credits. Recess and Internship are standalone periods. Recess courses default to 5 credits; adjust the course credits if needed.

Enter semester GPA: Enter a GPA from 0 to 5 and the total counted credits for each period. To combine with an existing CGPA, enter that CGPA and the credit units already included in it. Leave audited credits out.

3. Read your results

CGPA is a credit-weighted average. The course method calculates grade points from marks and weights each course by its credit units. The GPA-only method weights each entered semester GPA by its counted credits. The semester GPA card follows the current year and period you selected above.

4. Get a degree goal estimate

Choose a target class and deadline. Enter a typical regular-semester credit load and the number of Recess periods in your plan. Each estimated Recess period is counted as 5 credits. The recommendation is an estimate; confirm classification and retake rules with your programme.

5. Edit or remove entries

Use “Add semester” or “Add GPA entry” to create a blank entry, then choose its period and year where applicable. Use the × control on an entry to remove it. Your results are saved locally in this browser.
